BlackLazer is a shell company of [[RMAW]] utilized to divert funds to conduct military arms and technology development in secrecy. Officially its merely a miscellaneous tech company but that's far from its real scope of operations.

They were utilized to direct the [[Zeta Project]] in an [[RMAW Antarctic Research Facility (Ice Aground)| RMAW facility]] in the arctic. and as a result Developed [[The Gateway]] and several types of super-heated energy weapons
