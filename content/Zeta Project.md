Executives at RMAW voiced their demands due to concerns of rising energetic costs and sanctions placed on most sources of energy following the [[Ecologic crisis of 2004]]; thus they tasked multiple companies, including [[Black-Lazer]] with finding a solution.

The goal of the project being to find a private energy source that could circumvent government and worldwide regulations, as these were causing the operative costs of the parent company [[RMAW]] to skyrocket and making further operations unsustainable.

one of the secondary requirements given was making the energy source able to be feasibly privatized, and thus **BlackLazer** created the **Zeta Project**

The project took place in a [[RMAW Antarctic Research Facility (Ice Aground)| facility]] in the arctic
